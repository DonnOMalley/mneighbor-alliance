# Change Log

A plain-language summary of changes made in this branch, for anyone who isn't following the day-to-day edits. Entries are grouped by page or topic, newest at the top. This tracks *what changed and why it matters*, not line-by-line detail — see the git history if you need that.

---

## 2026-07-25 — Donn (Hello World)

### 🗺️ Neighborhood map & boundaries (Home page)

- Added an actual map of the neighborhood to the home page, showing exactly which streets mark the edges of Selbtoria.
- Set the official boundary streets: Marshall Avenue (north), Laurel Avenue (south), Chatsworth Street (west), and Grotto Street (east).
- Added a picture of the map next to the boundary list so it's easy to see at a glance, and it moves below the list automatically on phones.
- Fixed the spelling of the neighborhood name — it's **Selbtoria** (a mix of Selby + Victoria), not earlier misspellings that had crept into a few places.

### 🏘️ New page: Block Community Network

- Added a whole new guide explaining how individual blocks stay connected year-round — not just during big organized events.
- Introduced the volunteer role that anchors each block. We're calling this person a **"Trusted Servant"** instead of a "leader," to be clear that the role is about serving the neighborhood *and* yourself — not being in charge of anyone.
- Laid out a simple welcome process for greeting new neighbors when they move in.
- Set up a **Crime & Safety Committee** — a regular, informal meeting for neighbors to talk about safety concerns. It meets quarterly (every 3 months) instead of monthly, since most blocks won't have enough going on to need it more often. If something urgent comes up in between, a small group can form just to handle that specific issue and then disband once it's resolved.
- Clarified that this committee **monitors and reports on** police and city activity — it does not act as a formal go-between or partner of the police department.
- Added a simple, repeatable way for neighbors to ask for or offer help over the neighborhood chat — things like snow shoveling, wellness checks on neighbors, borrowing/sharing items, and urgent alerts.
- Laid out a yearly calendar of suggested block activities (spring cleanup, welcome visits, summer block party, a fall harvest/Halloween gathering, etc.), including flexibility for the August block party to scale up or down depending on how many volunteers are available.
- Changed how a block is covered when it doesn't have a volunteer yet — that responsibility is now shared across all three of the core volunteer roles instead of falling on just one person.

### 📚 New page: Community Resources Directory

- Added a one-stop reference page with city services, emergency and non-emergency contact numbers, housing help, senior resources, local organizations, parks, and transit info.
- Added the Summit-University Planning Council as a neighboring organization we may want to formally partner with, alongside the existing note about LexHam.
- Added a placeholder for links to our neighborhood group chats — the actual links will be added once the communities involved approve sharing them publicly.
- Adjusted phone numbers in tables so they no longer awkwardly break across two lines.

### 🎪 New page: Signature Programs

- Added a playbook of ready-to-run recurring events: a summer concert series, an ice cream social, movie nights in the park, and National Night Out — each with a simple description of what it takes to run it.

### 💌 New page: Welcome Info Sheet (template)

- Added a printable one-page handout that a block's Trusted Servant can hand to new neighbors, with local contact info, how to join the block group chat, and upcoming events.

### 🧹 Cleanup

- Fixed several links elsewhere on the site that were pointing to a personal computer file path instead of the correct page, which would have shown as broken links to anyone else visiting.
- Minor formatting consistency fixes across a couple of existing pages (headings, spacing) — no content changes.
